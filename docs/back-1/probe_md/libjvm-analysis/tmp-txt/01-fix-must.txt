## Doc 2: 13b-Compiler-Stubs-MH.md (19/50 → target 45+)

Covers JIT infrastructure — compiler threads, stubs, MethodHandle adapters. Current: 159 lines of surface description.

### Rewrite with:
1. **Production opener**: "`-XX:CICompilerCount=1` causes compile queue backlog → method stuck in interpreter → 100x slowdown → p99 latency spike."

2. **compilation_init walkthrough** (init.cpp + compileBroker.cpp):
   - Phase1 vs Phase2 split — why two phases (basic Infrastructure vs JVM-fully-initialized)
   - `make_compiler_thread()` with file:line — thread creation, entry function, queue polling loop
   - Compiler queue structure: `CompileTask`, `CompileQueue`, add/remove

3. **StubRoutines generate** (stubRoutines.cpp + stubGenerator_x86_64.cpp):
   - What stubs get generated: exception, deopt, safepoint, arraycopy, etc.
   - Why generate at startup (not first-use): avoid runtime allocation in signal context

4. **MethodHandle adapters** (methodHandles.cpp + methodHandles_x86.cpp):
   - `generate_adapters()` → `linkToStatic`/`linkToVirtual`/`linkToSpecial`/`linkToInterface` trampolines
   - What each trampoline does at CPU level (register setup, jump target)
   - Why 4 trampolines (vtable/itable/special dispatch differ)

5. **≥5 design rationales**:
   - Why stubs in CodeCache not in libjvm.so (dynamically generated, CPU-specific)
   - Why SoftRef clock uses os::javaTimeMillis not System.nanoTime
   - Why MethodHandle adapters generated at startup (not JIT-compiled lazily)
   - Why compiler threads are daemon (JVM exits during compile backlog)
   - Why ref processing deferred to Phase2 (GC infrastructure not ready in Phase1)

6. **GDB verification**:
   - Break at `compileBroker_init()`, print `_c1_count`/`_c2_count`
   - Break at `generate_adapters()`, dump adapter entry addresses
   - Verify stub addresses are in CodeCache range (not libjvm.so)

7. **Cross-reference**: Connect to 01's CodeCache section, 05's stubRoutines, 18's interpreter dispatch
